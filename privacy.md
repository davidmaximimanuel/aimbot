# AFRICAN INTELLIGENCE MODEL (AIM)

## Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** May 22, 2026

---

### 1. What AIM Is

AIM is a Telegram bot powered by artificial intelligence (Google Gemini API) and built to serve Nigerian and African users with culturally relevant assistance, memory, and local knowledge.

---

### 2. What Data We Collect

When you chat with AIM, we collect:

| Data | Purpose | Stored |
|------|---------|--------|
| Your Telegram user ID | To identify you and save your memory | Yes, in Supabase |
| Your username (if public) | To personalize greetings | Yes, in Supabase |
| Your messages & AIM's replies | To build your personal memory and improve context | Yes, in Supabase |
| Topics from your chats | To personalize news, recommendations, and recall | Yes, in Supabase |
| Your timezone/language preferences | To serve you better (e.g., "Africa/Lagos", Pidgin/English) | Yes, in Supabase |

---

### 3. What We Do NOT Collect

- Your phone number
- Your real name (unless your Telegram username reveals it)
- Payment information
- Messages from other chats or groups where AIM is not mentioned
- Location data (unless you explicitly share it in chat)

---

### 4. How We Use Your Data

- **Memory:** AIM remembers your past conversations so you don't have to repeat yourself.
- **Personalization:** News, reminders, and responses tailored to your interests.
- **Improvement:** Topic extraction helps AIM understand what matters to you.
- **No third-party sales:** We do not sell, trade, or share your data with advertisers or external companies.

---

### 5. Where Your Data Lives

Your data is stored in **Supabase** (PostgreSQL database), hosted on secure cloud infrastructure with encryption at rest. Access is restricted to the AIM service only.

---

### 6. Your Rights

| Right | How |
|-------|-----|
| See your data | Message AIM: "Show me my data" (feature coming soon) |
| Delete your data | Message AIM: "Delete all my memory" or contact the admin |
| Export your data | Contact the admin for a full dump of your chats |
| Opt out of memory | Message AIM: "Forget everything" — memory stops saving |

---

### 7. Third-Party Services

AIM relies on:

- **Google Gemini API** — processes your messages to generate replies
- **Supabase** — stores your chat history and profile
- **Railway** — hosts the bot 24/7
- **Logto** (optional) — for web dashboard authentication

These services have their own privacy policies. AIM does not control how they handle data at the infrastructure level, but we only send them what is necessary to operate.

---

### 8. Group Chats

When you mention `@askaimbot` in a group:

- AIM only sees the message you sent with the mention
- AIM does NOT read the rest of the group chat
- Replies are visible to the group unless you use ephemeral/translation features

---

### 9. Data Retention

- **Active users:** Data retained indefinitely until you request deletion
- **Inactive users:** Data may be purged after 12 months of no activity
- **Deleted data:** Permanently removed from Supabase within 7 days of request

---

### 10. Security

- All API calls use HTTPS/TLS encryption
- Supabase access restricted by service-role keys
- No plaintext passwords or tokens stored in code
- Regular security updates applied to hosting infrastructure

---

### 11. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, AIM will notify active users in-chat with a summary of changes. The latest version is always available by messaging AIM: "Privacy policy".

---

### 12. Contact

For data requests, complaints, or questions, contact the bot admin directly on Telegram.

---

### 13. Final Word

AIM was built for Africans, by Africans. Your data is your data. We respect that.
